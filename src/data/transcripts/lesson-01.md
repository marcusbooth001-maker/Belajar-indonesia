## 0. Welcome and what you'll learn

**Narrator:** Hey, and welcome to Lesson one of your Indonesian audio course. This one's for your ears only. Pop in your earbuds, go for a walk, or do a chore. There's nothing to look at.  
**Narrator:** You already understand a lot of Indonesian from home. Today is about getting it out of your mouth. So whenever you hear a pause, talk out loud. Really. Even if you're on the bus.  
**Narrator:** In the next half hour you'll learn about thirty-five words and phrases in three groups. First, greetings, so you can start a conversation. Second, introducing yourself, including the difference between casual and polite Indonesian. Third, talking about your family.  
**Narrator:** Each new item works like this. You hear the word slowly, then the English, then the word again. You repeat it in the pause. Then you hear it in a real sentence, and repeat that too. We'll keep coming back to old words, because that's how they stick.  
**Narrator:** By the end, you'll be able to have this conversation.  
**Ardi (example):** *Aku Dina. Aku berasal dari Auckland. Aku anak tunggal.*  
**Narrator:** I'm Dina. I come from Auckland. I'm an only child.  
**Narrator:** Ready? Let's go.

## Part 2 – Introducing yourself

**Narrator:** Now, introducing yourself. This is where Indonesian has two flavours. With friends and family you say *aku* and *kamu*. With teachers, adults, or someone you've just met, you say *saya* and *Anda*. Listen for it.

**Gadis (word):** *saya*

**Narrator:** I'm a new student.
